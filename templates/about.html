About Central Hub

Centralized Device & Issue Management System

Introduction

Central Hub is a centralized asset and maintenance management system designed for institutions to efficiently manage devices and equipment. The system provides a structured platform to monitor institutional assets, track maintenance activities etc.

Central Hub aims to eliminate manual record-keeping and scattered documentation by offering a unified digital solution. It enhances transparency, accountability, and operational efficiency within the campus environment.

Objectives

The primary objective of Central Hub is to create a reliable and organized system for managing institutional devices and handling maintenance issues effectively.

  • Maintaining a centralized database of devices and assets
  • Allowing staff to report device issues easily
  • Tracking maintenance progress and repair details
  • Monitoring repair costs and device history
  • Providing analytical insights for decision-making
  • Enhancing communication between departments and administration

User Management

  • Principal: Full administrative control and system monitoring
  • Staff: Can report issues and manage department-specific devices

Department Management

Departments can be created and assigned a teacher in charge. Devices are mapped to departments, ensuring structured asset control.

Device Management

  • Device type and specification
  • Brand and model
  • Cost and purchase details
  • Department and physical location
  • Status (Working / Reported)
  • Complete lifecycle tracking

Issue Management

  • Pending
  • In Progress
  • Solved

Repair details including repaired date, description, precautions taken, and maintenance cost are recorded for complete traceability.

Analytics & Reporting

  • Device working vs non-working distribution
  • Issue status distribution
  • Solved vs unsolved analysis
  • Maintenance cost tracking
  • PDF reports generation

System Workflow

  • A device is added and assigned to a department
  • Staff report issues when problems occur
  • Status updates as maintenance progresses
  • Repair details and costs recorded upon resolution
  • Historical data stored for analysis and auditing

Future Scope

  • Email notification system
  • Real-time alerts
  • Predictive maintenance analytics
  • Advanced financial reporting
  • Inventory threshold alerts

Conclusion

Central Hub transforms institutional asset management into a centralized, transparent, and intelligent system supporting efficient campus operations and sustainability.
